FAQ.

Common queries answered.

Can I customize my project package?

Absolutely. While our Lite and Core packages fit most standards, the Apex tier is explicitly designed for unique business configurations. We also customize aspects of Lite/Core to align with specific design goals.

What are the typical project timelines?

Startup/Lite pages usually deploy within 2-3 weeks. Growth/Core projects average 4-6 weeks depending on CMS integration and complexity. Apex systems have custom schedules based on project specs.

Do you assist with hosting setup and configuration?

Yes, we provide full support setting up domains, configuring DNS rules, installing SSL certificates, and provisioning server environments as standard service options on all service setups.

Is ongoing support included post-launch?

Every setup package includes a support period (1 month for Lite, 3 months for Core, lifetime critical for Apex). Additional retainer options are also available for continuous upgrades.
